Skydraw

One-tap space arcade for iPhone · Космическая аркада в одно касание для iPhone

Tap to break orbit and fly from planet to planet. Every run becomes a constellation with its own name, across eight galaxies of the real night sky. Free, works offline, no ads, no tracking.
